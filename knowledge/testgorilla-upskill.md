# Research Findings: Upskill 4.0 & TestGorilla Data Science Assessment

## 2. TestGorilla Candidate Experience (Verified)
- **Navigation**: STRICTLY ONE-WAY. Candidates CANNOT go back to previous questions once submitted or skipped. One question per screen.
- **Negative Marking**: NO negative marking / no penalty for wrong answers. TestGorilla explicitly advises guessing if unsure ("Better to score a point through luck, than to skip and guarantee no points").
- **Skipped Questions**: Counted as incorrect (0 points).

  - https://candidates.testgorilla.com/hc/en-us/articles/30851898009115-FAQ-Assessment-test-questions
  - https://www.testgorilla.com/blog/testgorilla-questions-answers/
